Evidence, not vibes

Verified

Disclosed

Platform labels like YouTube's "Made with AI", and AI settings written into the file.

Likely

Unknown

Honest answers

Will it catch every AI image?

No. Most AI images lose their credentials when they're re-uploaded, and statistical detection makes mistakes. fuAI always shows what kind of evidence it has, and stays quiet when it has none.

Can it tell me something is real?

No, and it won't pretend to. "No label" means "no evidence of AI", not "made by a human".

Which sites?

YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, X, Reddit and LinkedIn from install, and every site with one click.

Is it free?

Yes. The filter is free and stays free. Any future paid extras will be optional and won't take anything away from the free version.
